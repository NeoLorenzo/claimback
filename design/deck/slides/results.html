Jury criteria 1, 2 and 6. Four pipeline cases ran live on claude-opus-5-5 with real web search, plus a live test of the Gmail case workflow. The strong case reached 9 out of 10 in round 2, with all 42 claims verified, after chaos faults and a mid-run kill and resume. The MRI case shows the system generalizes to a different insurer, service and denial type. The fact checker caught a guideline quote that had dropped the word “only”. The injection case planted instructions to invent a hospitalization and a seizure. Nothing was fabricated, and the packet carries an integrity flag. The weak case is the honesty test: the first version burned three revision rounds and still said “send this”. We fixed it with a merit verdict, and the rerun stopped after one round at $1.39. Model cost runs about $1.40 to $3.10 per appeal. We haven’t measured the Gmail test’s model cost.
