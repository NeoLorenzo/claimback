Fighting one means paperwork, then rounds of review that can take six months. ProPublica and The Capitol Forum, “How Cigna Saves Millions by Having Its Doctors Reject Claims Without Reading Them” (March 2023): over two months in 2022, Cigna doctors denied more than 300,000 claims using its PXDX system, an average of 1.2 seconds each; Cigna called that description “incorrect”, and a class action followed; present it as reporting, not a court finding.
