Pricing is a hypothesis: a success fee on the value recovered, charged after the human-confirmed “overturned” outcome the case workflow already records; the rate is set in pilots. Constraint: with $1.39–$3.10 of model cost per appeal and win rates from about 1 in 3 (ACA claims) to 81% (Medicare Advantage prior auth), each win must cover the model cost of the losses; at about a 1-in-3 win rate a 1% fee breaks even only on claims worth about $900, before casework and support. Open questions: for pre-service denials like Rosa’s, the win is coverage, not cash, so “value recovered” must be defined (for example, the plan’s allowed amount) and collected from the patient; for clinics, a share of recovered revenue is simpler.
