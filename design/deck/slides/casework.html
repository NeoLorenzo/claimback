This is the “from task to workflow” part. A run takes about ten minutes, but a case takes weeks. Milestones 1 and 2 are built and tested against a real Gmail account: the case store, the IMAP and SMTP connector, the case worker with Message-ID dedupe, inbound redaction, the Correspondence agent, the quote and date validators, and the task and status logic. In a live test, the simulated insurer’s acknowledgment was kept as “not a decision”, and an evidence request became two tasks due October 29, 2026. Steps 4 to 6 are milestones 3 to 5, in progress: upload, the follow-up draft, Approve and send with the recipient allow-list, the case panel and notifications, and the outcome paths. Phone calls and reminders are roadmap, with the safeguards in Tom’s proposal.
