Customer

Clinic billing teams pay. Patients get the same help.

Who pays

Clinic billing and revenue-cycle staff

They appeal denials by hand every day, and every denial they overturn is revenue the clinic gets back. Hospitals spend $19.7B a year fighting denials, about $43.84 per claim before counting clinical staff time.

Who it helps

Patients and caregivers

They get a denial while they’re sick. To appeal, they have to find the plan clause, collect the evidence, write a formal argument and meet a deadline. First they need to know whether the appeal is worth it.

Why it’s worth fighting

Most denials are eventually paid

54.3% of private-payer denials end up paid, but only after about three rounds of review that take 45–60 days each. ClaimBack does that slow back-and-forth for the team.
