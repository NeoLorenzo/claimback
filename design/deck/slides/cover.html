Insurers deny 1 in 5 claims. Fewer than 1 in 100 denials are appealed.

ClaimBack is a team of Claude agents that turns a denial letter into a fact-checked appeal a person can sign and send. Then it keeps the case open, reads the insurer’s replies and works the case until there is an outcome.

Jury criterion 3, business viability: a real problem. KFF’s March 2026 analysis of CMS transparency data: HealthCare.gov insurers denied 19% of in-network claims in 2024, about 85 million claims, and fewer than 1% of denied claims were appealed. When people do appeal, it often works: in Medicare Advantage, 80.7% of appealed prior-authorization denials were fully or partially overturned in 2024. The gap is effort, not merit. ClaimBack closes that gap with a team of agents that writes the appeal and then keeps working the case for weeks.
