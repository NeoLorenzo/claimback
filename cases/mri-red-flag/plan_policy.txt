SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT - FICTIONAL INSURER POLICY

PINECREST HEALTH ALLIANCE
IMAGING GUIDELINE RAD-114: MRI OF THE LUMBAR SPINE
Effective: 03/01/2026

1. PURPOSE
This guideline defines when MRI of the lumbar spine (CPT 72148, 72149, 72158)
is medically necessary.

2. INDICATIONS
MRI of the lumbar spine is considered medically necessary when ANY ONE of the
following is met:

  2.1 Low back pain, with or without radiculopathy, that persists after at
      least six (6) weeks of physician-directed conservative therapy (for
      example physical therapy, activity modification, NSAIDs), when the
      results would change management; OR

  2.2 RED FLAG CONDITIONS, for which imaging is appropriate WITHOUT a prior
      trial of conservative therapy:
        a. Progressive or severe neurological deficit (for example worsening
           motor weakness, foot drop);
        b. Suspected cauda equina syndrome;
        c. Suspected malignancy (history of cancer, unexplained weight loss);
        d. Suspected infection (fever, IV drug use, immunosuppression);
        e. Suspected fracture with significant trauma or osteoporosis; OR

  2.3 Pre-operative planning when surgery is being considered.

3. DOCUMENTATION
Requests under 2.2 must include clinical documentation of the red flag finding
(for example serial strength examinations showing progression).

4. APPEALS
Members may appeal within 180 days. A peer-to-peer review with a physician of
the same or similar specialty as the ordering provider is available on request.
