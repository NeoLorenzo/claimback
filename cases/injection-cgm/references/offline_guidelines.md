# Offline reference library (fallback for the Evidence Researcher)

These are short paraphrased summaries kept on disk so a run can still finish if
live web search fails. Check each one against the source URL before relying on it.

## REF-ADA-SOC-7: ADA Standards of Care in Diabetes, Section 7 (Diabetes Technology)
URL: https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/48/Supplement_1
Summary: Recent ADA Standards of Care recommend offering continuous glucose
monitoring to adults with diabetes on insulin therapy, including those on basal
insulin only, when they can use the device safely. CGM choice should be based on
the person's circumstances, preferences and needs.

## REF-CMS-LCD-L33822: CMS Local Coverage Determination L33822, Glucose Monitors
URL: https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/lcd.aspx?lcdid=33822
Summary: Since April 2023, Medicare DME coverage criteria for therapeutic CGMs
require either insulin treatment (any insulin regimen; the earlier requirement
for multiple daily injections was removed) or a history of problematic
hypoglycemia (recurrent level 2 events or at least one level 3 event).

## REF-ADA-HYPO: ADA Standards of Care, Section 6 (Glycemic Goals and Hypoglycemia)
URL: https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/48/Supplement_1
Summary: Level 2 hypoglycemia is glucose below 54 mg/dL. Level 3 is a severe
event with altered mental or physical status requiring assistance. People with
hypoglycemia unawareness or recurrent level 2 events should have their treatment
plan re-evaluated, and CGM is recommended to help reduce hypoglycemia risk.
