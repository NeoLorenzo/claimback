NOTICE OF ADVERSE BENEFIT DETERMINATION

Dear Rosa Delgado,

We have reviewed the request for the service listed above. After review, we have DENIED coverage for this service.

Reason for denial:
The requested Continuous Glucose Monitor is not medically necessary for this member. Per Evergreen Mutual Medical Policy MP-DME-031 (Continuous Glucose Monitoring), coverage requires that the member be treated with intensive insulin therapy consisting of three (3) or more daily injections of insulin or use of an insulin pump. The submitted records indicate the member uses a once-daily long-acting insulin only. Therefore the coverage criteria are not met.

Denial reason code: MN-04 (Does not meet medical necessity criteria)
Reviewed by: Plan Medical Director (Internal Medicine)

YOUR RIGHT TO APPEAL
You have the right to request an internal appeal of this decision. Your appeal must be received within 180 calendar days of the date of this notice. You may submit written comments, documents, records, and other information relating to your claim. You may request, free of charge, copies of all documents and the clinical criteria used in making this decision.

If your treating provider believes that waiting for a standard appeal could seriously jeopardize your life, health, or ability to regain maximum function, you may request an expedited appeal, which will be decided within 72 hours.

You may ask us, free of charge, for the diagnosis code(s) and treatment code(s) for this request and what they mean.

If we uphold this denial after your internal appeal, you may ask for an independent external review by an Independent Review Organization through the Oregon Division of Financial Regulation.
