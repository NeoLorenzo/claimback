I. COVERAGE CRITERIA - PERSONAL (THERAPEUTIC) CGM

A therapeutic continuous glucose monitor and related supplies are considered
MEDICALLY NECESSARY when ALL of the following criteria are met:

  A. The member has a diagnosis of diabetes mellitus (type 1, type 2, or other);
     AND
  B. The member meets at least ONE of the following:
       1. The member is insulin-treated (any insulin regimen, including a single
          daily dose of basal/long-acting insulin); OR
       2. The member has a documented history of problematic hypoglycemia,
          defined as either:
            a. recurrent level 2 hypoglycemic events (glucose < 54 mg/dL) that
               persist despite multiple attempts to adjust medication or modify
               the treatment plan; OR
            b. a history of at least one level 3 hypoglycemic event
               (altered mental or physical state requiring third-party
               assistance);
     AND
  C. The CGM is prescribed in accordance with its FDA indications for use;
     AND
  D. The member or caregiver has been trained or will be trained to use the
     device, as documented by the prescribing practitioner; AND
  E. Within six (6) months prior to ordering the CGM, the treating practitioner
     has had an in-person or telehealth visit with the member to evaluate their
     diabetes control and determined that criteria A through D are met.

II. CONTINUED COVERAGE
Every six (6) months following the initial order, the treating practitioner
must have a visit documenting adherence to the CGM regimen and diabetes
treatment plan.

III. NOT MEDICALLY NECESSARY
CGM is not medically necessary when the criteria in Section I are not met.

EVIDENCE OF COVERAGE (EXCERPT) - SECTION 9: APPEALS

9.1 Internal appeals. A member, or a provider or authorized representative
acting on the member's behalf, may file an internal appeal within 180 calendar
days of receiving an adverse benefit determination. Standard pre-service
appeals are decided within 30 calendar days of receipt.

9.2 Expedited appeals. Where the standard timeframe could seriously jeopardize
the member's life, health, or ability to regain maximum function, an expedited
appeal will be decided within 72 hours.

9.3 Authorized representatives. A member may designate an authorized
representative in writing using the Plan's Appointment of Representative form.

9.4 External review. If the Plan upholds its denial on internal appeal, the
member may request independent external review through the state insurance
regulator within four (4) months of the final internal adverse determination.
